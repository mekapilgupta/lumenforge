The sole story

Softness you can see

Every pair starts with a hand-finished last, then a cushioned mid-layer and a flexible outsole that grips without bulk. The result is a shoe that feels broken-in from day one.

  • Anti-slip grip — textured TPR outsole tuned for wet tile and stone floors.
  • Breathable lining — soft, sweat-friendly and skin-kind.
  • Vegan-friendly upper — premium hand-feel, kinder materials.

Designed for Indian feet

Comfort science, not guesswork

We measured over 4,000 Indian women's feet to build lasts that fit properly — wider at the ball, gentler at the heel, kinder across the instep. Then we tested the pairs for 12-hour wear so you don't have to find out the hard way.

  • Wider, kinder last — comfort designed for real Indian sizing.
  • 12-hour wear tested — validated on real days, not just a treadmill.
  • All-day arch support — cushioning where it actually matters.
